# Agra

## Overview
Agra is a historic city in Uttar Pradesh, famous as the former capital of the Mughal Empire and home to three UNESCO World Heritage Sites. It is a primary stop on India's "Golden Triangle" tourist circuit, known globally for the iconic Taj Mahal.

## Best Time to Visit
- **Peak Season:** October to March (pleasant, cool weather)
- **Off-Peak/Monsoon:** April to September (scorching heat in summer; heavy rain and humidity in monsoon)

## Top Attractions
1. **Taj Mahal:** The world-renowned white marble mausoleum and monument to love.
2. **Agra Fort:** A massive red sandstone fortress and former imperial residence.
3. **Fatehpur Sikri:** An abandoned, perfectly preserved Mughal city located on the outskirts of town.
4. **Mehtab Bagh:** A garden complex offering stunning sunset views of the Taj Mahal from across the river.
5. **Itmad-ud-Daulah:** Often called the "Baby Taj," a beautiful tomb featuring intricate marble inlay work.

## Budget Guide (per person per day)
- **Budget (Backpacker):** ₹1,500 – ₹2,500 (hostels, street food, e-rickshaws)
- **Mid-range:** ₹3,000 – ₹6,000 (hotels, cafes, auto-rickshaws)
- **Luxury:** ₹8,000+ (resorts, private air-conditioned cabs)

## Local Food & Restaurants
- **Must try:** *Petha* (a translucent soft candy made from ash gourd), *Dal Moth* (spicy lentil mix), *Bedhai* with *Jalebi* (spicy breakfast bread), and various *Chaat* varieties.
- **Cost:** ₹100–₹300 for street food; ₹500–₹1,500 for a sit-down meal.

## Getting There & Local Transport
- **Getting There:** Agra is well-connected by train from Delhi (2–5 hours) and is accessible via the Yamuna Expressway by car (3.5 hours). The city has a small domestic airport with limited connectivity.
- **Local Transport:** E-rickshaws and auto-rickshaws are the primary modes of transport. Always negotiate fares in advance or use ride-hailing apps. The city also features a metro line connecting the Fort and the Taj Mahal.
