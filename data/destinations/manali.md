# Manali, Himachal Pradesh

## Overview
Manali is a high-altitude Himalayan resort town in India's northern Himachal Pradesh state. It has a reputation as a backpacking center and honeymoon destination. Set on the Beas River, it's a gateway for skiing in the Solang Valley and trekking in Parvati Valley.

## Best Time to Visit
- **Peak Season:** March to June (pleasant weather, 10°C to 25°C). Ideal for sightseeing and adventure sports.
- **Monsoon:** July to September (Heavy rainfall, landslides common). **Avoid** unless you specifically want to experience the lush green scenery and don't mind staying indoors.
- **Winter:** October to February (Snowfall, sub-zero temperatures). Best for snow lovers and skiing.

## Top Attractions
1. **Solang Valley:** Famous for adventure sports like paragliding, zorbing, and skiing (in winter).
2. **Old Manali:** Known for its cafes, bohemian vibe, backpacker hostels, and apple orchards.
3. **Rohtang Pass:** High mountain pass covered in snow year-round. Requires a special permit to visit.
4. **Hadimba Temple:** An ancient cave temple dedicated to Hidimbi Devi, surrounded by cedar forests.
5. **Vashisht Hot Springs:** Natural hot water springs believed to have medicinal properties.

## Budget Guide (per person per day)
- **Budget (Backpacker):** ₹1,500 - ₹2,500 (Hostels, local dhabas, public transport)
- **Mid-range:** ₹3,500 - ₹6,000 (3-star hotels, cafes, private cabs for local sightseeing)
- **Luxury:** ₹8,000+ (Premium resorts, fine dining, private guided tours)

## Local Food & Restaurants
- **Must try:** Siddu (local steamed bread), Trout fish, Tibetan momos, and Thukpa.
- **Top Cafes:** Cafe 1947, The Lazy Dog, Dylan's Toasted & Roasted Coffee House (all in Old Manali).
- **Cost:** A meal at a local dhaba costs ₹150-₹300. A meal at a good cafe costs ₹600-₹1000.

## Getting There & Local Transport
- **Nearest Airport:** Bhuntar Airport (Kullu) - 50 km away.
- **By Road:** Overnight Volvo buses from Delhi (approx 12-14 hours). Tickets range from ₹800 to ₹1,500.
- **Local Transport:** Auto-rickshaws are available but expensive. Renting a two-wheeler (scooty/bike) costs ₹500 - ₹1000 per day and is the best way to explore.
