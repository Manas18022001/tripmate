# Darjeeling

## Overview
Darjeeling is a historic hill station in West Bengal, famous for its colonial-era charm, sprawling tea plantations, and stunning views of the Kangchenjunga mountain range. It serves as a cultural melting pot of Nepali, Tibetan, and British influences and is a gateway to the Himalayas.

## Best Time to Visit
- **Peak Season:** March to May and October to November (pleasant, clear weather)
- **Off-Peak/Monsoon:** July to September (heavy rainfall and fog)

## Top Attractions
1. **Tiger Hill:** Famous for sunrise views over Kangchenjunga; entry fee approx. ₹50.
2. **Darjeeling Himalayan Railway:** A UNESCO World Heritage "Toy Train" offering scenic rides; tickets ₹1,200–2,200.
3. **Happy Valley Tea Estate:** Historic tea garden offering guided factory tours; entry approx. ₹100.
4. **Padmaja Naidu Himalayan Zoological Park:** Home to red pandas and snow leopards; entry approx. ₹50–100.
5. **Japanese Peace Pagoda:** A serene, tall monument dedicated to world peace.
6. **Batasia Loop:** A spiral railway track featuring a Gorkha War Memorial.

## Budget Guide (per person per day)
- **Budget (Backpacker):** ₹800–1,500 (hostels, street food, shared transport)
- **Mid-range:** ₹2,500–4,500 (hotels, cafes, local taxis)
- **Luxury:** ₹7,000+ (heritage resorts, private cabs)

## Local Food & Restaurants
- **Must try:** Momos (steamed dumplings), Thukpa (noodle soup), Tongba (local millet beer), and Darjeeling tea.
- **Cost:** ₹200–600 per meal at popular cafes like Glenary’s or Keventers.

## Getting There & Local Transport
- **Getting There:** The nearest airport is Bagdogra (IXB), 96 km away. The nearest major railhead is New Jalpaiguri (NJP). From both, shared terrain vehicles (taxis) are the most efficient way to reach the town (approx. 3 hours).
- **Local Transport:** Darjeeling is highly walkable. For longer distances or sightseeing, shared taxis and private cabs are readily available at stands near the town center.
