# Mumbai

## Overview
Mumbai, formerly Bombay, is India's commercial capital and a bustling, cosmopolitan metropolis known for its colonial architecture, vibrant street life, and the heart of the Bollywood film industry. Built on a series of islands, it is a city of sharp contrasts, blending historic heritage with a fast-paced, modern urban lifestyle.

## Best Time to Visit
- **Peak Season:** October to February (pleasant, lower humidity)
- **Off-Peak/Monsoon:** June to September (heavy rainfall, potential for flooding)

## Top Attractions
1. **Gateway of India:** Iconic waterfront monument built during the British Raj; free to view.
2. **Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Terminus (CSMT):** A UNESCO World Heritage site and architectural masterpiece; free to view exterior.
3. **Marine Drive:** A scenic 3.6 km promenade along the coast, perfect for sunset views; free.
4. **Elephanta Caves:** Ancient rock-cut cave temples on an island; requires a ferry ride (moderate cost).
5. **Sanjay Gandhi National Park:** A massive protected forest within city limits featuring the 2,400-year-old Kanheri Caves; entry fee applies.
6. **Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Vastu Sangrahalaya:** The city's premier art and history museum; entry fee applies.

## Budget Guide (per person per day)
- **Budget (Backpacker):** ₹2,000–₹3,000 (hostels, street food, local trains/buses)
- **Mid-range:** ₹5,000–₹8,000 (hotels, cafes, occasional taxis/autos)
- **Luxury:** ₹15,000+ (premium hotels, private cabs, fine dining)

## Local Food & Restaurants
- **Must try:** Vada Pav, Pav Bhaji, Bhel Puri, Irani Chai with Bun-Maska, and fresh seafood (Konkan style).
- **Cost:** Street food meals are very affordable (₹50–₹200), while mid-range restaurant meals typically cost ₹500–₹1,500.

## Getting There & Local Transport
- **Getting There:** The city is served by Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International Airport (BOM) and is a major hub for the Indian Railways with multiple terminals (CSMT, Mumbai Central, Bandra Terminus).
- **Local Transport:** The "Local" suburban train network is the city's lifeline for north-south travel. For shorter distances, use the expanding Metro lines, auto-rickshaws (allowed only in suburbs beyond Bandra/Sion), or app-based cabs (Uber/Ola) for convenience and transparent pricing.
