# Andaman and Nicobar Islands

## Overview
The Andaman and Nicobar Islands are a remote archipelago in the Bay of Bengal, known for their pristine beaches, coral reefs, and dense tropical forests. While politically part of India, the islands offer a unique, laid-back atmosphere, primarily serving as a premier destination for scuba diving, snorkeling, and nature exploration.

## Best Time to Visit
- **Peak Season:** Mid-January to mid-May (sunny weather, calm seas, ideal for diving)
- **Off-Peak/Monsoon:** Late May to July (frequent rain, strong winds, rough seas)

## Top Attractions
1. **Cellular Jail:** A historic colonial-era prison in Port Blair.
2. **Radhanagar Beach:** Famous for its white sands and clear waters on Havelock Island.
3. **Mahatma Gandhi Marine National Park:** A protected area near Wandoor ideal for glass-bottom boat rides and snorkeling.
4. **Ross Island:** Known for its British-era ruins and historical significance.
5. **Neil Island:** A quieter alternative to Havelock, featuring natural rock formations and beautiful beaches.

## Budget Guide (per person per day)
- **Budget (Backpacker):** ₹2,500 – ₹4,000 (hostels/homestays, street food, government ferries)
- **Mid-range:** ₹6,000 – ₹10,000 (hotels, cafes, private ferries, auto-rickshaws)
- **Luxury:** ₹15,000+ (resorts, private cabs, guided diving tours)

## Local Food & Restaurants
- **Must try:** Fresh seafood (tandoori fish, crab, prawns) and local Indian coastal cuisine.
- **Cost:** Basic meals are affordable; seafood and resort dining are priced at a premium.

## Getting There & Local Transport
- **Getting There:** The primary entry point is Veer Savarkar International Airport (IXZ) in Port Blair, with flights from the Indian mainland. Long-distance passenger ships are available from Kolkata, Chennai, and Visakhapatnam.
- **Local Transport:** Inter-island travel is primarily via government or private ferries. Within islands, auto-rickshaws and taxis are common, while renting a scooter is the most popular way to explore Havelock and Neil Island.
