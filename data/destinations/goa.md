# Goa

## Overview
Goa is a state in western India with coastlines stretching along the Arabian Sea. Known for its pristine beaches, vibrant nightlife, Portuguese heritage, and seafood, it caters to both party-goers and those seeking a relaxing coastal retreat.

## Best Time to Visit
- **Peak Season:** November to February (Cool and pleasant, 20°C to 30°C). Packed with tourists, highest prices, famous for festivals like Sunburn and Christmas/New Year parties.
- **Summer:** March to May (Hot and humid, 25°C to 35°C). Good for swimming and avoiding huge crowds.
- **Monsoon:** June to October (Heavy rains). Beautiful lush greenery, but most beach shacks are closed and swimming in the sea is strictly prohibited.

## Top Attractions
1. **North Goa Beaches:** Baga, Calangute, and Anjuna (Famous for water sports, shacks, and nightlife).
2. **South Goa Beaches:** Palolem, Colva, and Agonda (Quieter, cleaner, ideal for relaxation).
3. **Dudhsagar Waterfalls:** A majestic four-tiered waterfall accessible via a jeep safari or trekking.
4. **Old Goa Churches:** Basilica of Bom Jesus and Se Cathedral (UNESCO World Heritage sites).
5. **Fort Aguada:** A 17th-century Portuguese fort offering panoramic ocean views.

## Budget Guide (per person per day)
- **Budget (Backpacker):** ₹2,000 - ₹3,000 (Hostels in Anjuna/Vagator, street food, rented scooty)
- **Mid-range:** ₹4,000 - ₹8,000 (Beachfront huts, shack dining, occasional clubbing)
- **Luxury:** ₹12,000+ (5-star beach resorts like Taj Exotica, fine dining, private yachts)

## Local Food & Restaurants
- **Must try:** Goan Fish Curry, Pork Vindaloo, Bebinca (dessert), and Feni (local cashew liquor).
- **Top Places:** Curlies (Anjuna), Thalassa (Siolim), Gunpowder (Assagao), Martin's Corner (Betalbatim).
- **Cost:** A seafood meal at a beach shack costs ₹500-₹1200. Premium dining costs ₹1500-₹3000+.

## Getting There & Local Transport
- **Airports:** Dabolim (GOI) in South Goa and Mopa (GOX) in North Goa.
- **By Train:** Madgaon (MAO) and Thivim are major railway stations connecting to Mumbai and Delhi.
- **Local Transport:** Renting a scooty is essential (₹400 - ₹600 per day). Taxis are notorious for being extremely expensive and unmetered. GoaMiles is the local taxi app, but availability can be spotty.
