# Leh

## Overview
Leh is the high-altitude capital of Ladakh, situated in the Indus River Valley at the crossroads of ancient Himalayan trade routes. It is a rugged, culturally rich destination known for its Buddhist monasteries, dramatic mountain landscapes, and as a base for high-altitude trekking.

## Best Time to Visit
- **Peak Season:** June to September (pleasant weather, accessible mountain passes)
- **Off-Peak/Monsoon:** November to March (extremely cold, many services closed)

## Top Attractions
1. **Shanti Stupa:** A modern, iconic white-domed Buddhist monument offering panoramic views of the valley (Entry: ₹30).
2. **Leh Palace:** A historic 17th-century royal palace overlooking the city (Entry: ₹25).
3. **Thiksey Monastery:** A stunning, well-maintained Geluk monastery featuring a two-storey-tall Maitreya Buddha statue (Entry: ₹50).
4. **Hall of Fame:** An informative war museum run by the Indian Army showcasing regional history and military operations.
5. **Namgyal Tsemo Gompa:** A 15th-century monastery perched high above the palace (Entry: ₹20).
6. **Confluence of Zanskar and Indus:** A scenic spot where the distinct brown and blue rivers meet.

## Budget Guide (per person per day)
- **Budget (Backpacker):** ₹1,500 – ₹2,000 (hostels, local buses, street food)
- **Mid-range:** ₹3,500 – ₹5,000 (guesthouses, cafes, shared taxis)
- **Luxury:** ₹8,000+ (resorts, private cabs, guided tours)

## Local Food & Restaurants
- **Must try:** Thukpa (noodle soup), Momos (dumplings), and local butter tea.
- **Cost:** ₹100–₹300 for a typical meal at local Tibetan or Punjabi-style restaurants.

## Getting There & Local Transport
- **Getting There:** The most reliable way is by flight to Kushok Bakula Rimpochee Airport (IXL) from Delhi. Overland travel is possible via the Manali-Leh or Srinagar-Leh highways (June–October), though these routes are long and require acclimatization.
- **Local Transport:** Leh is compact and largely walkable. For nearby sites, public buses are available from the main terminal, while private taxis can be hired for day trips. Motorbikes are popular for local exploration but require careful handling due to high altitude.
