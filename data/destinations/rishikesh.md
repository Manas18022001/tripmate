# Rishikesh, Uttarakhand

## Overview
Rishikesh is the "Yoga Capital of the World," situated in the foothills of the Himalayas along the banks of the sacred Ganges River. It is a major pilgrimage town for Hindus and a hotspot for adventure sports enthusiasts, backpackers, and spiritual seekers.

## Best Time to Visit
- **Peak Season:** September to November and February to May (Pleasant weather, 15°C to 30°C). Best for rafting and outdoor activities.
- **Monsoon:** June to August (Heavy rainfall, river rafting is banned). Waterfalls are spectacular but trekking is risky due to landslides.
- **Winter:** December to January (Cold, 5°C to 15°C). Great for yoga retreats but too cold for water activities.

## Top Attractions
1. **Laxman Jhula & Ram Jhula:** Iconic suspension bridges over the Ganges. Laxman Jhula is currently closed for repairs but remains a landmark.
2. **Triveni Ghat:** Famous for the evening Ganga Aarti ceremony (free to attend).
3. **White Water Rafting:** Grade 3 and 4 rapids on the Ganges. 16 km stretch costs ₹1,000-₹1,500 per person.
4. **Bungee Jumping:** India's highest bungee at Jumping Heights (83 meters). Costs ₹3,550 per person.
5. **Beatles Ashram (Maharishi Mahesh Yogi Ashram):** Where the Beatles stayed in 1968. Entry fee ₹150.
6. **Neer Garh Waterfall:** A short trek from Laxman Jhula area. Entry fee ₹30.

## Budget Guide (per person per day)
- **Budget (Backpacker):** ₹800 - ₹1,500 (Ashram stays, local food, shared activities)
- **Mid-range:** ₹2,500 - ₹5,000 (Riverside hotels, cafe dining, adventure sports)
- **Luxury:** ₹8,000+ (Luxury resorts like Aloha on the Ganges, private yoga sessions)

## Local Food & Restaurants
- **Must try:** Aloo Puri (street breakfast), fresh fruit smoothies, Israeli/Middle Eastern food (large Israeli backpacker community), and Maggi at mountain viewpoints.
- **Top Cafes:** Little Buddha Cafe, The Sitting Elephant, Freedom Cafe, Bistro Nirvana.
- **Cost:** A meal at a backpacker cafe costs ₹200-₹500. Street food costs ₹50-₹100. Note: Rishikesh is a strictly vegetarian and alcohol-free city.

## Getting There & Local Transport
- **Nearest Airport:** Jolly Grant Airport (Dehradun) — 35 km away.
- **By Train:** Rishikesh and Haridwar railway stations are well-connected to Delhi (5-6 hours).
- **Local Transport:** Walking is the best way to explore the main areas. Shared autos to Haridwar cost ₹30. Scooty rentals available for ₹300-₹500 per day.
