# Kerala (Munnar, Alleppey & Kochi)

## Overview
Kerala, known as "God's Own Country," is a state in southern India famous for its backwaters, tea plantations, pristine beaches, and Ayurvedic traditions. A typical Kerala trip covers Kochi (history & culture), Munnar (hill station & tea gardens), and Alleppey (backwater houseboats).

## Best Time to Visit
- **Peak Season:** October to March (Post-monsoon, lush green, 20°C to 32°C). Best weather for all activities.
- **Monsoon:** June to September (Heavy rainfall). Great for Ayurvedic treatments and discounted hotel rates, but outdoor activities are limited.
- **Summer:** April to May (Hot and humid, 30°C to 36°C). Munnar remains cool and pleasant even in summer.

## Top Attractions
1. **Alleppey Backwaters:** Overnight houseboat cruise through Kerala's famous backwater canals. Costs ₹6,000-₹15,000 per night for a private houseboat (2-4 people).
2. **Munnar Tea Gardens:** Rolling hills covered in tea plantations. Visit the Kanan Devan Tea Museum (₹125 entry).
3. **Fort Kochi:** Colonial-era area with Chinese fishing nets, art galleries, and cafes.
4. **Periyar Wildlife Sanctuary (Thekkady):** Boat safari to spot wild elephants and sambar deer. Entry + boat ride ₹300.
5. **Varkala Beach:** Dramatic cliffside beach, much quieter than Goa beaches.

## Budget Guide (per person per day)
- **Budget (Backpacker):** ₹1,500 - ₹2,500 (Homestays, local meals, public buses)
- **Mid-range:** ₹4,000 - ₹7,000 (Resorts, houseboat stay, auto-rickshaws)
- **Luxury:** ₹12,000+ (Premium houseboats, Ayurvedic spa resorts, private drivers)

## Local Food & Restaurants
- **Must try:** Kerala Sadya (banana leaf feast), Appam with Stew, Karimeen Pollichathu (pearl spot fish), Kerala Parotta with beef fry, Payasam.
- **Top Places:** Kayees Rahmathulla Hotel (Kochi, best biryani), Paragon Restaurant (Calicut-style), Dal Roti (Fort Kochi).
- **Cost:** A Kerala Sadya (traditional thali) costs ₹150-₹250. Seafood meals at restaurants cost ₹400-₹800.

## Getting There & Local Transport
- **Airports:** Cochin International Airport (COK) for Kochi/Munnar, Trivandrum Airport (TRV) for southern Kerala.
- **By Train:** Ernakulam Junction (Kochi) is a major railway hub.
- **Local Transport:** KSRTC buses are reliable and cheap (Kochi to Munnar ₹200, 4-5 hours). Auto-rickshaws use meters in Kochi. Renting a car with driver for inter-city travel costs ₹2,500-₹3,500 per day.
