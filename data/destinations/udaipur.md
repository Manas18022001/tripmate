# Udaipur

## Overview
Known as the "City of Lakes," Udaipur is a romantic destination in Rajasthan famous for its stunning palaces, intricate architecture, and serene water bodies. It serves as a cultural hub, offering a blend of royal history and vibrant Rajasthani traditions.

## Best Time to Visit
- **Peak Season:** September to March (pleasant, cool weather ideal for sightseeing)
- **Off-Peak/Monsoon:** April to June (very hot); July to September (humid)

## Top Attractions
1. **City Palace:** A massive complex of palaces, museums, and gardens overlooking Lake Pichola. (Entry: ₹400 for museum, ₹30 for grounds)
2. **Lake Pichola:** An iconic artificial lake featuring boat rides and views of the Lake Palace.
3. **Jagdish Temple:** A large, beautifully carved Indo-Aryan temple dedicated to Lord Vishnu.
4. **Fateh Sagar Lake:** A scenic lake featuring three islands, including Nehru Park and the Solar Observatory.
5. **Bagore-ki-Haveli:** A historic waterfront mansion turned museum, famous for its evening cultural dance shows. (Entry: ₹100)

## Budget Guide (per person per day)
- **Budget (Backpacker):** ₹1,500 – ₹2,500 (hostels, street food, local transport)
- **Mid-range:** ₹3,500 – ₹6,000 (hotels, cafes, auto-rickshaws)
- **Luxury:** ₹10,000+ (heritage resorts, private cabs)

## Local Food & Restaurants
- **Must try:** Dal Baati Churma, Mirchi Bada, Pyaz Kachori, and Gatte Ki Sabzi.
- **Cost:** ₹150 – ₹500 per meal depending on the venue.

## Getting There & Local Transport
- **Getting There:** Accessible via Maharana Pratap Airport (25 km from city) and a well-connected railway station with trains from Delhi, Mumbai, and Jaipur.
- **Local Transport:** The old city is best explored on foot. For longer distances, auto-rickshaws are the primary mode of transport; app-based cabs (Uber/Ola) are also available.
