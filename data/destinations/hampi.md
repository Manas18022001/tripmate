# Hampi

## Overview
Hampi is a stunning UNESCO World Heritage site in Karnataka, featuring the sprawling ruins of the 14th-century Vijayanagara Empire. The landscape is defined by a surreal terrain of millions of ancient boulders, lush palm groves, and historic temples along the Tungabhadra River.

## Best Time to Visit
- **Peak Season:** October to February (pleasant, cool weather for exploring ruins).
- **Off-Peak/Monsoon:** April to September (extremely hot in summer; heavy rains in monsoon).

## Top Attractions
1. **Virupaksha Temple:** A still-active, historic temple dedicated to Lord Shiva.
2. **Vithala Temple:** Famous for its iconic Stone Chariot and musical pillars.
3. **Lotus Mahal & Elephant Stables:** Exquisite examples of Indo-Islamic architectural fusion.
4. **Matanga Hill:** The best vantage point for panoramic views and sunsets over the ruins.
5. **Royal Enclosure:** A vast complex featuring the Mahanavmi Dibba platform and ancient step wells.
6. **Hemakuta Hill:** A collection of early temples offering a great sunset spot near the main bazaar.

*Note: A combined entry ticket (approx. ₹600 for foreigners) covers the Vithala Temple, Lotus Mahal, and Archaeological Museum.*

## Budget Guide (per person per day)
- **Budget (Backpacker):** ₹1,000–₹1,500 (hostels, local buses, street food).
- **Mid-range:** ₹2,500–₹4,000 (guesthouses, cafe meals, daily auto-rickshaw hire).
- **Luxury:** ₹6,000+ (resorts, private car rentals).

## Local Food & Restaurants
- **Must try:** South Indian staples like Idli, Dosa, and Gunta Ponganalu; "Hello to the Queen" dessert.
- **Cost:** ₹150–₹400 per meal at local cafes.

## Getting There & Local Transport
- **Getting There:** The nearest railway station is **Hosapete Junction (HPT)**, 13 km away, connected by overnight trains from Bengaluru, Hyderabad, and Goa. The nearest airport is Vidyanagar (VDY).
- **Local Transport:** Renting a bicycle or scooter is the best way to explore at your own pace. Auto-rickshaws are available for hire (negotiate rates beforehand). Frequent local buses run between Hospet, Kamalapur, and Hampi.
