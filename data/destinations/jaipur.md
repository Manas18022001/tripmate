# Jaipur, Rajasthan

## Overview
Jaipur, the Pink City of India, is the capital of Rajasthan. Known for its stunning forts, palaces, vibrant bazaars, and rich Rajasthani cuisine, it forms one corner of India's famous Golden Triangle tourist circuit (Delhi - Agra - Jaipur).

## Best Time to Visit
- **Peak Season:** October to March (Cool and dry, 10°C to 25°C). Perfect weather for exploring forts and outdoor sightseeing.
- **Summer:** April to June (Extremely hot, 35°C to 45°C). Avoid unless you enjoy intense heat. Hotel prices drop significantly.
- **Monsoon:** July to September (Hot and humid with occasional rain). The city looks greener but can be uncomfortable.

## Top Attractions
1. **Amber Fort:** A massive hilltop fort with stunning views and mirror-work interiors. Elephant rides available (₹1,100 per ride).
2. **Hawa Mahal:** The iconic Palace of Winds with 953 small windows. Entry fee ₹50 for Indians.
3. **City Palace:** A blend of Mughal and Rajasthani architecture. Entry fee ₹200.
4. **Nahargarh Fort:** Best sunset point in Jaipur overlooking the entire city. Entry fee ₹50.
5. **Jantar Mantar:** An astronomical observation site and UNESCO World Heritage Site. Entry fee ₹50.
6. **Johari Bazaar & Bapu Bazaar:** Famous for traditional Rajasthani jewelry, textiles, and handicrafts.

## Budget Guide (per person per day)
- **Budget (Backpacker):** ₹1,200 - ₹2,000 (Hostels, street food, public buses)
- **Mid-range:** ₹3,000 - ₹5,500 (Heritage hotels, restaurant dining, auto-rickshaws)
- **Luxury:** ₹10,000+ (Palace hotels like Rambagh Palace, private guided tours)

## Local Food & Restaurants
- **Must try:** Dal Baati Churma, Laal Maas (spicy mutton curry), Ghewar (sweet), Pyaaz Kachori, Lassi.
- **Top Places:** Laxmi Mishthan Bhandar (LMB) on Johari Bazaar, Rawat Mishthan Bhandar (best kachori), Tapri Central (rooftop cafe).
- **Cost:** A thali at a local restaurant costs ₹150-₹300. Street food snacks cost ₹30-₹80 each.

## Getting There & Local Transport
- **Airport:** Jaipur International Airport (JAI) with direct flights from all major Indian cities.
- **By Train:** Jaipur Junction is well-connected. Trains from Delhi take 4-5 hours.
- **Local Transport:** Auto-rickshaws are the primary mode (negotiate fares, typical ride ₹50-₹150). Ola/Uber available. City buses exist but are crowded.
